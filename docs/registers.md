# Registers and records

An Indian retail outlet is run on registers. Inspections (OMC officer, Legal Metrology, PESO) are register-reading exercises, and a dealer's protection is a complete, signed, same-day record.

Sources for each item are in [sources.md](sources.md).

## 1. Operational registers (daily)

### Nozzle / shift register
- Opening and closing totaliser for every nozzle, every shift.
- The next shift's opening must equal the previous closing; any jump is a red flag that must be resolved before sales start.
- Gross litres = closing − opening. Net litres = gross − testing litres.
- Signed by the attendant and the supervisor.

### Shift settlement (cash book of the forecourt)
Per attendant/DU, per shift:
- Net litres and value per product.
- Collection split: cash, card/POS, UPI/QR, credit.
- Expected vs counted cash → **short/excess**, signed by the attendant.
- Card and UPI slips attached.

### DSR — Daily Sales Register / Daily Sales Report
The day-level roll-up: all shifts, all products, plus lubricants, plus stock. Generated at day close and frozen. Section-by-section structure in [reconciliation.md](reconciliation.md). There is no single government-mandated layout — each OMC expects daily sales and stock records and inspectors compare them against your tank dips and meter readings.

### Density register
- Density of MS/HSD measured **at 15 °C** before commencing daily sales, recorded in the format of MDG Annexure 1.
- Includes temperature and the conversion working, not just the number.
- Also the daily **water dip** record (water presence in the tank, in cm, with action taken).
- Fuel receipt densities at decantation are recorded here or in the receipt register.

### Test record (5-litre measure)
- MDG 1.4.2 requires the dealer to check the Weights & Measures seals and certificate and to **test every nozzle each day** using a W&M-verified 5-litre measure.
- Record: nozzle, measure serial, observed quantity, variation, action, signature.
- Testing fuel is returned to the tank and therefore deducted from meter litres in the DSR.

### Tank dip register
- Physical dip (in mm) before and after every decantation, and at day close for every tank.
- Dip is converted to litres using the tank **calibration chart** — not a linear guess.
- Provides the physical stock that book stock is tested against.

### Receipt / tank-lorry register
- Tank lorry number, invoice/challan, product, compartment-wise quantity, density and temperature, seals, sample drawn/retained, before/after dips, decanted quantity, short-delivery (if any).
- Short delivery at decantation is a claim against the transporter/OMC and must be documented the same day, with samples.

### Complaint register
- Kept on the forecourt, available to customers and inspectors (MDG). Customer name/contact, nature of complaint, action, closure.

### Price-change record
- On a retail price revision (06:00), stock on hand is revalued and the difference settled with the OMC by debit/credit note.
- Keep the revision circular, the stock statement at the moment of revision, and the note.

## 2. Financial records

| Record | Purpose | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash book | All cash in/out, day-wise | Daily |
| Bank deposit slips / passbook | Cash and cheque deposits | Daily/next morning |
| Payment acquirer statements | UPI, card, fleet-card settlements and MDR | Weekly match |
| Purchase invoices from OMC | Fuel purchases (VAT invoice), commission credit notes | Per delivery |
| Sales register (VAT) | Fuel sales by product and rate, for VAT return | Monthly |
| GST sales register | Lubricants, shop, services (CGST/SGST/IGST split) | Monthly |
| Credit ledger (khata) | Credit customers, vehicle numbers, ageing | Daily posting, weekly review |
| Expense vouchers | Electricity, salary, maintenance, shortage written off | Daily capture, monthly posting |
| Stock register | Book stock vs physical, gain/loss with reasons | Daily DSR, monthly reconciliation |
| Asset register | Dispensers, tanks, canopy, generator, with depreciation | Annual |
| Staff/attendance and salary register | Wages, PF/ESI if applicable | Monthly |

## 3. Statutory documents to keep on file

- Dealership agreement and amendments.
- PESO licence (Class A/B petroleum storage) and fire-safety certificate.
- Legal Metrology verification/stamping certificates for each dispensing unit and measure.
- Weights & Measures and OMC seal records.
- Pollution/consent certificate, shop licence, FSSAI (if food is sold).
- GST registration certificate, VAT/TIN, PAN, trade licence.
- Labour registrations (PF, ESI, shops & establishments) as applicable.
- Insurance policies (stock, fire, public liability).

## 4. What an inspection usually checks

Consistent across MDG-based inspections and dealer guidance:

- Daily Sales Report and density register up to date, for the last several months.
- Test records for every nozzle, every day, with the W&M measure available for checking.
- Complaint register present and available on the forecourt.
- Stock/sales records reconciled, with variance within permissible limits and reasons recorded.
- Price board, authorised price display, complaint box and MDG posters displayed.
- Dispenser seals intact, zero shown before delivery, nozzle-wise product labelling correct.
- Clean toilets, air, water, telephone/facilities available.

Record-keeping failures are treated as irregularities in their own right, separately from quantity/quality offences — so a tidy register is cheaper than an argument.

## 5. Practical format advice

- **One sheet per shift, one page per day.** Anything that requires opening a laptop mid-shift gets abandoned during the evening rush.
- **Sign at the moment of counting.** An attendant signature obtained the next morning is worth nothing in a dispute.
- **Photograph nothing-critical.** Cash slips and credit vouchers are photographed at the counter and attached to the shift; paper originals stay in the file.
- **Never back-date a frozen day.** Reopen with an explicit adjustment entry that carries its own reason and approver, so the audit trail survives.
